Dataset Description

The Credit Score Classification dataset contains financial and personal information about customers and is designed to classify their credit scores into three categories: Good, Standard, and Poor.

The dataset includes various attributes related to customers' income, occupation, credit history, loans, credit cards, outstanding debt, payment behavior, and delayed payments.

Key Information

Dataset: Credit Score Classification

Task: Multi-class Classification

Target Variable: Credit_Score

Target Classes: Good, Standard, Poor

Data Type: Structured tabular data

Use Case: Credit score prediction and financial risk analysis

This dataset can be used to develop and evaluate machine learning classification models for predicting a customer's credit score based on their financial and credit-related information.

Source

The dataset is available on Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/parisrohan/credit-score-classification
